This video was created for the Jacksonville Zoo Video Competition. A small team filmed Malayan tigers on location and interviewed their caretakers face-to-face. The COVID Chronicle

This 10-minute documentary was created for the 2025 UNF Scholastic Video/Film Contest and explored the impact of COVID on schools and learning during and after the pandemic. We interviewed students and teachers about their experiences and how the school system adapted to an unprecedented time. The piece was entered in the 2025 contest.

  • Project Type: Documentary
  • My Role: Camera operation, audio, interviewing, and editing
  • Production: Camera, audio, interviews, and post production
